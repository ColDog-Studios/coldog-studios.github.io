## Be Ready Before Something Breaks

Incident readiness helps your organization prepare for security events and disruptive technical issues before they become chaotic. Response support helps bring structure to containment, recovery, and follow-up hardening when something has already happened.

This work is focused on practical preparation, clear steps, and technical follow-through.

## Common Needs

-   Incident response planning
-   Backup and recovery review
-   Logging and alerting recommendations
-   Access review after suspicious activity
-   Containment and recovery support
-   Post-incident review and hardening

## What the Work Can Include

-   Review current response procedures and technical visibility
-   Identify gaps in logging, backups, and administrative access
-   Create or improve incident handling checklists
-   Help investigate scope and affected systems
-   Support containment and recovery tasks
-   Document lessons learned and hardening steps

## Deliverables

-   Readiness checklist
-   Response plan notes
-   Recovery and hardening recommendations
-   Post-incident summary when applicable
