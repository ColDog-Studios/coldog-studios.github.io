## Prepare for Reviews and Requirements

Compliance and readiness work helps translate security requirements into practical technical and operational steps. It is useful when you need to prepare for vendor reviews, customer questionnaires, internal requirements, or audit-style evidence collection.

The focus is readiness, clarity, and remediation support.

## Common Needs

-   Control gap assessments
-   Vendor or customer security questionnaire support
-   Policy and procedure review
-   Evidence collection planning
-   Remediation tracking
-   Security documentation cleanup

## What the Work Can Include

-   Review requested controls or questionnaire items
-   Map requirements to current systems and processes
-   Identify missing evidence or incomplete controls
-   Recommend realistic remediation steps
-   Help organize documentation and response materials
-   Track open items through completion

## Deliverables

-   Gap summary
-   Evidence checklist
-   Remediation tracker
-   Recommended response language or documentation notes
