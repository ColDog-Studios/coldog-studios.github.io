## Practical Security Guidance

Cybersecurity consulting helps you understand where your environment stands, which risks matter most, and what can be improved with the time, budget, and tools you already have.

This work is project-based and focused on clear recommendations, practical remediation, and documentation your team can use after the engagement.

## Common Needs

-   Security posture reviews
-   Vulnerability review and remediation planning
-   Security policy and process guidance
-   Account, access, and permissions review
-   Endpoint and server hardening recommendations
-   Security roadmap planning

## What the Work Can Include

-   Review current systems, configurations, and processes
-   Identify high-impact risks and practical improvement areas
-   Prioritize findings by business impact and effort
-   Recommend technical and procedural controls
-   Help implement selected improvements when hands-on work is needed
-   Document decisions, findings, and next steps

## Deliverables

-   Findings and recommendations
-   Prioritized remediation plan
-   Configuration notes or implementation documentation
-   Follow-up review items for future improvement
